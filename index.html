Studio Publisher

Studio Publisher is the private publishing tool of Lola's Window, an independent creator making short original animated videos about Philippine folklore.

It uploads and schedules our own finished videos to our own accounts: YouTube (@lolaswindow), TikTok and Instagram (@lolas.window). It has one user, the channel owner, and is not offered to the public.
