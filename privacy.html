Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Studio Publisher is a private tool used only by the creator of Lola's Window to upload and schedule our own videos to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in. Studio Publisher uses YouTube API Services and the TikTok Content Posting API.

What we access

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to our channel and to read our channel's basic details and our videos' status and statistics.
  • TikTok: permission to read our account's basic profile, upload or publish our own videos, and read our own videos' statistics.

What we store

  • The sign-in tokens Google and TikTok give us, kept on our own computer and readable only by our account.
  • Video IDs, publishing times and view counts for our own videos, refreshed regularly and kept to plan future posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in TikTok under Settings > Security > Apps and services. When access is removed we delete the stored tokens and the data above.

Platform policies

Studio Publisher's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of TikTok follows the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Developer Terms.
